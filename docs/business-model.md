# Business Model: Wood Treatment Facility Scheduling and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7521`
- ISCO-08: `7521`
- Occupation: Wood Treaters
- Social impact: worker-safety, environmental-safety, industrial-continuity

## Customer

- treatment-facility operators / wood-preservation contractors
- independent wood-treatment crews / crew cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- batch/inventory/progress-record logging
- preservative-chemicals/timber-stock supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to shop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a treatment-execution decision (e.g. deciding
  to proceed with a specific wood-treatment run), ever
- no direct finalization of a chemical-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring a treated batch safe for handling or shipment), ever
- no override of a shop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (chemical exposure, ventilation failure,
  equipment condition) always route to human sign-off, regardless of
  confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
